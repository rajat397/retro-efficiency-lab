# Sprint plan — 2026-11-13 (experiments, then a steer review)

**Sprint goal.** Convert the product thesis (`docs/PRODUCT-THESIS.md`) from a document into
*running artifacts*, and find out which parts of it survive contact with measurement.

**Sprint shape.** A small number of experiments, each with a hypothesis, a method, and a
falsifier that is stated **before** the result is known. Each experiment ends as a card
status change plus a bench in the repo — never as a claim in prose.

**Sprint review (end of sprint).** The steer direction is reviewed against what the
experiments showed, not against what the thesis hoped. Agenda in §5.

**Rules inherited from the repo.** Deterministic metrics are load-bearing; wall-clock is
labeled noisy; naive baselines are documented as *good-faith idiom*, not strawmen; every
number is counted, and relative error on a near-zero quantity is not a number; honesty
lines are ledgers and get audited.

## 1. The experiment set

| # | experiment | hypothesis | falsifier | depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** | **Unit of work** (M-15) — **DONE 2026-11-13** | The budget unit that kills runaways without killing legitimate work is **not** a per-task rate — it is defined at the *outcome* level, with per-task caps only as a backstop | a per-task unit matches the outcome-level unit on both axes (waste **and** false kills) across the whole threshold sweep | — |
| E1′ | **→ follow-up spawned by E1** | The product's L1 primitive is a **pair**: a cheap consumption ceiling + a *progress signal*; and a progress signal is an **observability** requirement, not a metering one | real fleets expose no usable progress/stall signal, so the primitive cannot be built | E1 |
| **E2** | **Ledger** (M-15b) | Spend reconstructed after the fact is **not** equivalent to spend attributed at admission; on workloads with retries and shared sub-work, reconstruction misattributes | post-hoc reconstruction equals admission-time attribution on every task | E1 |
| **E3** | **Arrival shape** (T9→generalize) | The burst factor κ is **not** universal across workloads — it varies by work type, so one fleet's shape cannot size another fleet | κ is statistically indistinguishable across workload segments | — |
| **E4** | **Governor harness** (L2/L3) | A governor that leases concurrency+rate, runs AIMD when the quota cannot be leased, jitters retries, and hard-kills on a task budget, beats naive **against a live peer** on wall-clock and on spend | naive matches or beats the governor on either axis against the mock provider | E1 (budget unit) |
| **E5** | **Degradation ladder** (M-12 bp-2) | The ladder's order is **not fixed** — it is a computation over measured per-rung quality-vs-cost curves; the steer's fixed order (cheaper model → fewer sub-agents → shorter context → hard stop) is optimal only for some workloads | the fixed order is optimal for every workload tested | E1 |
| **E6** | **Determinism matrix** (CI) | Every bench's deterministic counters reproduce on **three** platforms, and the ones that don't are honestly labeled | all benches already reproduce on three platforms with no relabeling | — |

**Not in this sprint, deliberately:** real partner traffic (blocked on input), the
**quality-floor contract** (needs a quality metric we do not yet have — E5 measures the
*decision rule*, not the quality curves), and anything requiring a commercial binary

## 2. Sequencing

```
E1 ──► E2        (the ledger's unit is E1's unit)
  │
  ├────► E4      (the governor needs a budget unit to hard-kill on)
  └────► E5      (ladder rungs are budgets)

E3 ────────────► (independent; mines existing local traffic)
E6 ────────────► (independent; CI plumbing)
```

**E1 first.** It is the deciding experiment: its result sets the unit used by E2, E4 and
E5, and it is the one primitive (L1) that three of the four product layers depend on.

**E1 outcome (2026-11-13):** H2 **CONFIRMED** (no consumption unit separates the two kinds —
false kills are forced by the cost distribution, exactly, at every cap); H1 **REFUTED** (the
outcome-level governor defers work, it does not discriminate); the discriminating signal is
**progress**, reaching the same completions and the same false kills at **4.5× less waste**
than the best consumption unit, but losing at very tight waste ceilings because detecting a
stall costs turns. So the primitive is a **pair**, and E2/E4/E5 now depend on E1′ (measure
the progress signal in a real fleet) rather than on a unit choice.

## 3. Definition of done, per experiment

1. A bench that runs standalone (`node bench.mjs`), exits 0, prints one `VERDICT:` line of
   deterministic counters.
2. **The bench has teeth** — mutating the load-bearing rule makes it exit non-zero. (Run #1's
   lesson: a bench that cannot fail is theater.)
3. A card (or a card status change) whose claim is *the measured result*, with the honest
   limits in the same breath.
4. One additive row in `docs/LEARNINGS.md`.
5. Any deviation from the plan recorded where it happened, not discovered later.

---

## 4. Sprint review agenda (end of sprint)

1. **Steer direction.** Is the problem still the right one? Check the thesis's own test:
   does a different big problem map onto the fundamentals *structurally*? If yes, replace
   the thesis rather than defend it.
2. **E1's verdict.** If the unit is outcome-level, the product's ledger and budget are
   designed around outcomes — that changes L1, L2, and the product pitch. If it is
   per-task, the pitch is simpler and the false-kill risk is real and must be priced.
3. **Layer status.** Which of L0–L4 now exist as running artifacts, and which are still
   prose? (Target: L1 and L3 real; L2 real; L4 a capability plan, not a UI.)
4. **What the experiments killed.** Every hypothesis that failed stays in the repo as a
   failed claim with its falsifier — that is the point of writing falsifiers first.
5. **The next sprint.** Chosen from the gaps the experiments opened, not from the plan.

---

## 5. Definition of done, sprint

- Every experiment in §1 that was attempted has a committed bench + a card status change
  (or an explicit "infeasible, here is why").
- `docs/PRODUCT-THESIS.md` §5 (the falsifiable-claims table) is updated to the measured
  state of each claim — **every row updated, including the ones that failed**.
- `docs/PITCH-STRESS-TEST.md` is audited for stale claims.
- The steer direction is reviewed and recorded, with the decision and its rationale in
  `docs/decisions.md`.
